- **(S3b draft, finding for the S5 covenant-reviewer and the release gate) Stale-input deadline.** `close()` writes T' = max(T + round_len, own + d_min) with own = the newest *input's* creation DAA (`controller.sil:473-484`, check 20), not the DAA of the close itself. A close sent more than d_min after its newest input therefore opens a round whose deadline has already passed: every shard is created at or after T', so shard check 6 (`require(OpTxInputDaaScore(0) < deadline)`) refuses every buy, and the round has no sales but still needs a close and a draw. Anyone may close, so a closer who submits late with old inputs can cause one dead round (a liveness and griefing issue, not solvency); the round after an overrun is exposed because its close waits for the late draw.
