Status: approved 2026-10-13 (owner). Design references are to `docs/design.md` v0.4.1 with the M7 edit applied. rusty-kaspa paths are relative to `refs/rusty-kaspa-v2.1.0`, SilverScript to `refs/silverscript`.

Interview answers (2026-10-13):
- **Jackpot, duplicated jackpot combination, held second prize:** first answer "local proof plus luck", amended the same day. They are **forced on a private simnet node** the project runs on the owner's PC as the only miner, with the shipping scripts. The anchor block is ground there so that chosen tickets win (A5b, D111). On testnet-10 they are still attempted by luck. No test-only game or seed variant.
- **Backers:** four more testnet checks:
  - the liability lock on a full withdrawal by the genesis backer;
  - the minimums refused;
  - a double settlement refused;
  - every conversion and payout equal to the library model (D121).
- **Indexer:** a program in this repo that runs on the owner's PC for the campaign, reads public testnet-10 nodes and writes local files. It is not trusted: everything built from its data is checked on-chain against the frozen roots (D112).
- **Launch bundle:** a JSON file under `docs/launch/`, committed and pushed. kascov.io preflight may be used in review; it receives public testnet data only (D113).
- **Pacing:** a fast campaign of about 3 hours. The retention cases are built strictly offline from the saved copies. A 30-hour soak then runs unattended and repeats one draw and one claim after the public nodes have deleted the data. The soak is the last checkbox (D114).

## A0. Part 1: the whale-margin floor (first task, D109)

`set_params()` does not carry the margin (`params.sil:89-164`), so it needs no change and no test.

Tests that assert numbers depending on the margin are re-derived, not loosened, and each changed expectation is listed in the S1 DECISIONS row. `spike_vdf`'s margin table keeps its own explicit margins.

## A1. Decisions (each becomes a DECISIONS row)

- **D110:** `MARGIN_MIN = 15,000` on-chain (A0). The value is provisional until the research session reports (D108, D109).
- **D111: jackpot, duplicated jackpot and held second prize go on a private simnet** (interview, amended). These cannot be forced on testnet-10: the seed is the anchor block's hash, and covering every combination costs 285,012–570,024 tKAS at m = 2. On a private simnet node with no peers, the project is the only miner. Facts from `refs/rusty-kaspa-v2.1.0`:
  - simnet has `skip_proof_of_work = true` (`consensus/core/src/config/params.rs:737`; the check is skipped at `consensus/src/pipeline/header_processor/pre_ghostdag_validation.rs:102-105`);
  - the block hash covers the nonce and the timestamp (`consensus/core/src/hashing/header.rs:7-35`);
  - simnet runs at 10 BPS with finality depth 432,000 (`params.rs:742`, `config/bps.rs:92-94`), so seed_offset, F and VOID_MIN mean what they mean on testnet-10;
  - Toccata rules hold from genesis on every network (`consensus/src/pipeline/virtual_processor/processor.rs:643-655`; OpChainblockSeqCommit has no network gate, `crypto/txscript/src/opcodes/mod.rs:1389-1404`).

  Grinding takes a block template, varies the nonce, hashes the header locally (`hashing::header::hash`), maps X.hash through the seed (D94), and submits only the block whose seed makes a chosen held combination win (`submit_block`, `rpc/core/src/api/rpc.rs:132`; `--enable-unsynced-mining`, `kaspad/src/args.rs:339`).

  What it proves: a real node's mempool and consensus accept the shipping scripts' jackpot, duplicate and second-prize draws and claims. It also demonstrates D100's bias concretely: a sole miner chooses X.hash for one hash per try. It does not measure mainnet cost. On testnet-10 the cases are still attempted by luck.
- **D122: the safety rule** in CLAUDE.md goes from "Local script-engine tests and testnet-10 only" to "Local script-engine tests, a private simnet node on this PC with no peers, and testnet-10 only". The change is made at the start of S4, with the owner's approval of this spec. The mainnet hook stays as is: a command naming kaspad must carry `--simnet` (`.claude/hooks/block-mainnet.js:19-30`).
- **D121: backer checks on testnet** (A5 C2–C6):
  - a 9.99 KAS deposit is refused (MIN_DEPOSIT, `vault.sil:52`);
  - a partial withdrawal that leaves less than wmin is refused (`vault.sil:325-338`);
  - a full withdrawal by the genesis backer while round 2's jackpot is fixed is capped at draw 2 (`drawer.sil:836-859`). It settles in PAY mode with a carried request (`request.sil:223-278`), and that request is converted and settled FINAL at draw 3;
  - a second `settle()` of a spent request is refused;
  - every conversion and settlement value equals `game::convert` / `game::settle` on the state decoded by `RoundResult::from_tx` (`game.rs:950-984`, `:1057-1108`; `draw.rs:1074`).
- **D112:** the indexer (A4) is untrusted, run by the project, and file-based. It needs a node with utxoindex and the v2 RPC. A copy from any source works if it reproduces the frozen roots.
- **D113:** the bundle goes in `docs/launch/tn10-m8.json`. kascov.io preflight is used for review on the testnet transactions (no keys, no `kaspa:` data). sil-debug (`refs/silverscript/debugger/cli`, binary `cli-debugger`) runs locally.
- **D114: the retention criteria.**
  - The fast campaign shows the late-draw overrun (A5 C5): round 2's `draw()` is 60 minutes late, round 3's sales pause after its deadline, and round 3 is drawn and claimed from the indexer copy.
  - The fast campaign builds the wallet and indexer claims strictly offline from the stores.
  - The soak (A5 C8) is a round whose purchases are more than 30 h old when its `draw()` and a claim are built, after a public node is shown to no longer return the round's earliest purchase. The round runs long because its `close()` is late, not because a predecessor's draw is: a real `draw()` can only be at most 12 h late, and a 24-hour void would add a day.
- **D115: the m change goes downward.** Genesis starts round 1 at m = 3 (genesis checks only the range, `genesis.rs:272`). The testnet vault is far too small for m = 3 (that needs about 1.49 M KAS at margin 1.5), so `close()` of round 1 chooses m = 2 by the game-size rule. Raising m would need a vault of roughly 300,000 KAS or more.
- **D116: the below-threshold referral** follows from the admin change. `set_params()` in round 1 lowers s_R from 1,000 to 500 bp. In round 2, b = 1 gives r = 0.05 KAS < M_REF = 0.1 KAS (`shard.sil:38,257`): with a referral output it is refused, without one it is accepted. b = 2 gives r = 0.1 KAS and is paid. Note: round 1's own `close()` already uses the new s_R in its m choice (`controller.sil:401-403`), and round 1's `draw()` pays the fee to round 2's fee address (`drawer.sil:948-950`). Both are design behaviour, and the campaign asserts them.
- **D117: fees and budgets.**
  - The fee rate comes from the node's `get_fee_estimate` normal bucket, at least 100 sompi/gram (design §9). Fee = rate × the builder's exact mass.
  - The compute budget per v1 input comes from the existing builder sizing.
  - The fixed 0.5 KAS buy fee and the fixed `probe.rs` fee are not used by the campaign.
  - The facts behind the fee are verified in `refs/` (the RPC model and the mempool policy) before S3 and cited in the row.
- **D118: file formats.** JSON via `serde_json` 1.0.151, which is already in `Cargo.lock`. Byte fields are lowercase hex; integers are JSON numbers (all below 2⁵³, checked when written).
- **D119: the testnet instance parameters** (A2).
- **D120: shapes not exercised on testnet.** Not submitted: `absorb` (a pool past its 7-day deadline), `return_deposit_after` (T + 14 days) and `return_deposit_with_pool` (7 days). The jackpot and second-prize pools are on simnet only (D111). Local evidence only: M4 and M7. The campaign does not wait 7 days. `refresh()` of one ticket is submitted in the soak (D_IDLE = 24 h, `ticket.sil:45`).

## A3. State layouts

**On-chain: unchanged.** No state field, template or argument changes in M8. Part 1 changes one constant and one comparison in `controller.sil` and in `drawer.sil`.

**Off-chain** (D118; every file starts with `"format": "<name>/1"`, and readers refuse an unknown format):

**`index/1`**: the indexer store, `tn10-data/<cov-hex>/index.json` plus an append-only `index-log.jsonl`. Log line:
```
{"seq": n, "kind": "add"|"remove", "chain_block": hex32, "chain_daa": u64,
 "txid": hex32, "tx": <kaspa_consensus_core::tx::Transaction serde JSON>, "accepted_daa": u64}
```
- `remove` lines come from `removed_chain_block_hashes`.
- `index.json` is a snapshot rebuilt from the log: `cov`, `genesis_txid`, `last_chain_block`, `rounds[]` and `live_utxos[]`.
  - Each round: `round`, `m`, `close_txid`, `marker_daa`, `roots[K]` (frozen), `draw_txid`, `record`, the `RoundResult` fields (`x_hash`, `mk_daa`, `void`, winning numbers, pools), and `buys[]`.
  - Each buy: `txid`, `shard_index`, `combos[b]` as 6-byte hex, `ticket_outpoint`, `ref`.
  - Each live UTXO: outpoint, role, round, value, `covenant_id`, state hex.

**`wallet/1`**: `tn10-data/<cov-hex>/wallet-r<round>.json`.
- `round`, `m`, `shards[K]`: each an ordered list of leaf updates (`combo`, `count_after`) and the root after each.
- `frozen_roots[K]`, checked equal to `close()`'s roots.
- `tickets[]`: outpoint, bundle, b, claimed bitmap, payout class and key.
- `record`: the round result record bytes, once drawn.

The wallet writes an entry only after the purchase is chain-accepted. It is written by the runner (the only buyer); it never reads `index/1`.

**`campaign/1`**: `tn10-data/<cov-hex>/campaign.json`.
- `step` (the next step ID of A5), `genesis` outpoints, the per-step `txids[]` with the node URL used, the result per check, the DAA and wall time per step.
- Re-running `campaign resume` continues at `step`. A step's transactions are submitted only if they are not already chain-accepted (idempotent by txid).

**`bundle/1`**: `docs/launch/tn10-m8.json`.
- `network`: `testnet-10`.
- `toolchain`: the rusty-kaspa rev, the SilverScript rev, the compiler output hash per template.
- `genesis_txid`, `genesis_outpoint`, `authorized_outputs[]`: index, value, script hex, covenant binding.
- `covenant_id`, recomputed by `genesis::covenant_id`.
- `templates[]`: role, preimage hex, hash.
- `params`: every genesis value of A2.
- `live_utxos[]` at publication, with the DAA and chain block of the snapshot.

## A4. Components

**`lottery::index`** (pure, no I/O, no RPC):
- `classify(tx, templates, cov) -> Option<Event>`. It identifies the transition by the covenant role of input 0 and its entry signature. Events: Genesis, Buy, SetParams, Close, Draw (including void), Claim, RequestDeposit, RequestWithdraw, Settle, Refresh, ReturnDeposit, Absorb, and Unknown for a covenant-bound transaction it cannot parse. Unknown is an error the indexer reports; it never skips silently.
- `parse_buy` reads shard index, b, bundle and referral from the shard input's pushes and the ticket output state. `parse_close` reads the frozen roots and m′. `parse_draw` reuses `RoundResult::from_tx`.
- `Rebuild::apply(event)` / `revert(txid)` maintain shard trees per round (`smt::Tree`) in shard UTXO chain order, the rounds and the live UTXO set.
- At `Close`, the rebuilt root of every shard must equal the frozen root. If it doesn't, the round is marked `incomplete` and no proof is offered from it.

**Offline builders** (`lottery`): `ClaimCase::from_store(&wallet_or_index, ticket, funding)` and `DrawCase::from_store(&index, anchor_proof, funding)`. They take only a store, the anchor proof and funding UTXOs. Their signature has no RPC client, so offline building is enforced by type, not by convention.

**`tn10 indexer`** (long-running):
- It polls `get_virtual_chain_from_block_v2` with full transaction verbosity from a start hash (the chain block before genesis). It keeps the transactions that spend an instance UTXO or create an output bound to the instance covenant ID, and writes `add`/`remove` log lines.
- It checks pruning: at each poll it notes the oldest round whose earliest buy block a node no longer returns (`get_block` fails or has no body). The soak uses this as evidence.
- After a restart it resumes from `last_chain_block`. If that block is no longer known (offline more than the pruning window), it stops with an error instead of skipping.
- Before S3, the facts behind the v2 call (field names, batch size, verbosity) are re-read in `rpc/core/src/model/message.rs` and `rpc/service/src/service.rs`.

**`tn10 campaign <start|resume|status>`**: runs A5 step by step and writes `campaign/1` after every step. It checks each expected refusal by the node's rejection reason text. **`tn10 bundle`** writes `bundle/1`; **`tn10 bundle-verify <file>`** recomputes the covenant ID and template hashes, compares the live UTXOs with the node, and exits non-zero on any mismatch.

**Submission.** Every campaign transaction goes through `submit_transaction` (orphans disallowed) to a public testnet-10 node from the resolver or `TN10_WRPC_URL`. It is never sent to a miner or a private endpoint. Each txid is logged with the node URL. The mainnet guards in `net.rs` stay.

## A5b. Private simnet: forced jackpot, duplicate and second prize (D111, D122)

**Node.** An in-process kaspad from the pinned rev, in the pattern of `refs/rusty-kaspa-v2.1.0/testing/integration/src/daemon_integration_tests.rs:98-134`:
- simnet, `--utxoindex`, `--enable-unsynced-mining`;
- no peers and no DNS seeding (`kaspad/src/daemon.rs:567-570`);
- a Borsh wRPC server on 127.0.0.1 only;
- a fresh data directory under `crates/target/simnet-data/` per run.

`crates/simnet` is a workspace member but not a default member, so `cargo test` never builds RocksDB. Its network guard accepts only simnet and `kaspasim:` addresses and refuses anything else, the counterpart of `tn10/src/net.rs`.

Prerequisites: LLVM/clang with `LIBCLANG_PATH`, and protoc (`refs/rusty-kaspa-v2.1.0/README.md:71-78`). They are installed with the owner's approval at the start of S4. Command: `cargo run -p simnet --release -- <sn1|…|sn5|all>`.

**Mining.**
- The runner mines blocks on demand: `get_block_template` to a simnet address, then `submit_block`. One block at a time, so the chain is linear.
- Lottery transactions go through `submit_transaction` into the node's mempool and are mined from templates. They are never inserted into a block by hand.
- Timestamps come from the template (≤ now + 132 s, `pre_ghostdag_validation.rs:38-42`).
- Round windows are counted in blocks, so a round takes seconds.

**Grinding.**
- When the next template's DAA score is the first ≥ D (its selected parent below D), the runner varies the nonce, hashes the header locally and computes the seed and the winning numbers (D42, D94).
- It submits the first candidate whose result gives the wanted tier to a chosen held combination, then mines 50 more blocks. It builds the 272-byte proof through the same `get_seq_commit_lane_proof` path as `tn10` (made network-generic) and submits `draw()`.
- Expected tries: about C = 142,506·m for one target combination, one header hash each.
- The runner logs tries, wall time, and the result an honest first candidate would have given (the miner's choice made visible, SN5).

| ID | Round setup | Ground for | Checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| SN1 | m = 3 (round 1), J from the genesis vault; a single holding of combination c | c = jackpot | the jackpot pool equals J; the vault pays J_house and the boost pot pays J_boost; the holder's claim pays all of J; afterwards L ≤ cap × V |
| SN2 | m = 2; combination c in two tickets on different shards (h = 2) | c = jackpot | both claims pay floor(J / 2); the remainder stays in the pool; a third claim (same ticket again) refused |
| SN3 | m = 3; the same 5 main numbers held with two different bonuses, the jackpot bonus not held | second prize | n2 = 2, pool = 2 × G2, J_house stays in the vault; both claims pay G2 |
| SN4 | m = 2; jackpot and second prize both held; a backer's full withdrawal requested in the round | jackpot + second prize | the vault pays J_house + G2 × n2 ≤ L; the withdrawal conversion after the hit equals `game::convert` and keeps L′ |
| SN5 | any SN round | — | tries and time recorded; the ungrounded first candidate gives a different result; D100 evidence row (simnet, sole miner; not a mainnet cost) |

All values are checked against the library model (`DrawCase::expected`, `game::convert`, `game::settle`). Results go in a "Simnet" section of `docs/tn10-mass-check.md`.
